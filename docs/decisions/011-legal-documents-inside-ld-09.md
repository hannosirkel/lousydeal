# 011 — The legal documents are written inside LD-09

**Status:** accepted, 2026-09-06. **Operator's decision.**

## The conflict

[`fresh-build.md`](../working/fresh-build.md) §23 says: *do not write terms,
refund policy, VAT configuration or consumer disclosures as part of any LD
slice.* LD-09 rows V8 to V11 wrote four of them.

`brand.md` §5 recorded that as being at the operator's instruction. V10a's Gate
D found the instruction existed nowhere a reader could check, which made §23 —
the document the qualified reader is handed — forbid the thing they are being
asked to review.

## The decision

The instruction is real and is recorded here.

§23's rule protects against a slice inventing consumer disclosures as a side
effect of shipping a feature. LD-09 is not that: it is the slice whose whole
subject is what the site says, and the four documents are its deliverable
rather than a by-product. Writing them anywhere else would have meant a
storefront with a footer linking four routes that did not exist.

## What it does not change

§23's **gate** is untouched. Drafting is not advice, nothing publishes until a
qualified human reader closes the gate, and the open items are listed at the
foot of [`ld-09-visual-identity.md`](../working/ld-09-visual-identity.md).

The rule stands for every other slice. LD-02 may send the confirmation email
§ 55 requires; it may not decide what that email says about withdrawal without
the same gate.
